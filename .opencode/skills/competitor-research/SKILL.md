# Competitor Research

Owned by `product-strategist`. Feeds `business-plan.md` and SEO/keyword work.

## Workflow

1. Identify 3–5 direct competitors and 2 aspirational references.
2. For each, capture: audience, positioning, key features, pricing, tone, site structure.
3. Note gaps and opportunities Skynet's site can exploit.
4. Summarize differentiation in one paragraph.

## Output

```markdown
| Competitor | Audience | Positioning | Notable features | Pricing |
|-----------|----------|-------------|------------------|---------|
```

## Rules

- Use public information only.
- Do not copy competitor copy; extract patterns, not text.
- Every claim of differentiation must map to a feature we will actually build.
