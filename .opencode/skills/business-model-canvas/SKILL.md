# Business Model Canvas

Owned by `product-strategist`. Output: `docs/projects/<id>/business-plan.md`.

## The nine blocks

1. **Customer segments** — who the site serves.
2. **Value proposition** — the core promise.
3. **Channels** — how customers arrive.
4. **Customer relationships** — how they are retained.
5. **Revenue streams** — how money is made.
6. **Key resources** — what is needed to deliver.
7. **Key activities** — the critical work.
8. **Key partners** — third parties that help.
9. **Cost structure** — main costs.

## Rules

- Keep it to one page; clarity over completeness.
- Tie every block to the requirements and audience.
- Flag assumptions that need validation.
