# Status Reporting

Owned by `project-manager`.

## Report format

- **Overall** — on track / at risk / blocked.
- **Completed** — since the last report.
- **In progress** — with owners.
- **Blocked** — item, blocker, needed action.
- **Next** — the immediate critical-path steps.
- **Decisions needed** — from the master or user.

## Cadence

- After each phase gate.
- On any status change to blocked.
- On request from the master.

## Rules

- Lead with the overall status.
- Be specific; link artifacts.
- No status without evidence (artifact or test result).
- Keep it short enough to read in under a minute.
