# Copywriting

Owned by `content-writer`.

## Principles

- Lead with the benefit, not the feature.
- Write at a grade-8 reading level unless the audience demands otherwise.
- One idea per sentence; short paragraphs.
- Use active voice and concrete language.
- Address the reader as "you".

## Workflow

1. Read the audience, value proposition, and page goal.
2. Draft a headline, subhead, and body for each section.
3. Add a clear call to action per section.
4. Cut filler; tighten every line.
5. Check against the brand voice guide.

## Rules

- No lorem ipsum in deliverables.
- Every page has one primary CTA.
- Claims must be true and specific.
- Match the brand voice exactly.
