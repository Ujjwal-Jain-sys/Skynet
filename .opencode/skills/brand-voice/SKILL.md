# Brand Voice

Owned by `content-writer`. Output: `docs/projects/<id>/content/brand-voice.md`.

## Define

- **Personality** — 3–5 adjectives (e.g., warm, expert, direct).
- **Tone** — how it flexes by context (marketing vs. error messages).
- **Do** — sentence patterns, vocabulary to use.
- **Don't** — clichés, jargon, words to avoid.
- **Examples** — 3 good and 3 bad samples.

## Rules

- All copy across pages follows the guide.
- Error and empty states stay human, never robotic.
- Consistency over cleverness.
- Revisit only with the master's approval (it is a contract).
