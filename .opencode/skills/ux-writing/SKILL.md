# UX Writing

Owned by `content-writer` with `ui-design`.

## What to write

- **Labels** — clear, scannable, consistent.
- **Buttons** — action verbs ("Create account", not "Submit").
- **Errors** — say what happened and how to fix it; no blame.
- **Empty states** — explain and offer a next action.
- **Confirmations** — state the consequence of destructive actions.
- **Onboarding** — short, benefit-focused.

## Rules

- Be concise; every word earns its place.
- Use sentence case consistently.
- No jargon, no humor in errors.
- Keep terminology consistent across the product.
- Every string is real copy; no placeholders.
